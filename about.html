About Lil Fish Community Center

Nestled in the heart of a small farming and fishing town, Lil Fish Community Center has served as a gathering place for families, celebrations, and community traditions since 1926.

What began as a small town meeting hall has grown into one of the community’s most beloved event spaces — hosting weddings, seasonal festivals, live music, graduations, anniversary celebrations, and unforgettable local events.

What The Center Is Used For Today

Today, Lil Fish Community Center continues to bring people together through weddings, concerts, holiday markets, harvest festivals, retirement parties, reunions, graduation celebrations, and community events throughout the year.

With flexible indoor and outdoor event spaces, modern amenities, and small-town charm, the center remains a cornerstone of the Lil Fish community.
